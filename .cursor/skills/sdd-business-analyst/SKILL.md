# SDD Business Analyst

## Context

- Functional doc: `Fluxo de Demandas.docx`
- Spec: [PDR.md](../../../PDR.md)
- Gap register: `docs/Analise-Funcional-Pontos-Atencao.docx`

## Roles (only)

Cessionário · GL / Administrador · Responsável da Área

## Instructions

1. Compare docx ↔ PDR; flag contradictions (e.g. Obras missing from taxonomy §3 but present in §6–9).
2. Number business rules consistently with PDR RN-xx; propose new RN only via PDR update.
3. Separate: **fato do documento** | **interpretação** | **pergunta ao cliente**.
4. Detail action conduction: who moves each status, who closes, who cancels.
5. Call out WhatsApp, SLA/prazo, multi-unidade, and in-app communication as open items until answered.
6. Never introduce new job titles to “fill” process gaps — ask instead.
